FIND YOUR WAY IN

THERE’S A PLACE FOR YOU HERE.

Whether you want to volunteer, explore a new discipline, or take on a role with the core team, start where you are. You don’t need to fit one particular background to contribute.

01 · Volunteer Track

Be a volunteer.

Open to people from any background. You could be a high-school student looking to earn volunteer hours, a college student building experience, or simply someone with time, curiosity, and a desire to contribute.

  • No specific academic background required
  • Explore a team and contribute at your pace
  • Receive a certificate on successful completion
  • Eligible volunteers can receive a founder’s letter of recommendation (LOR)
Apply to volunteer ↗
02 · Core Team Track

Work with the core team.

Want to take on a defined responsibility and help shape the work we do? Explore the open functional roles and apply to become part of the leadership and execution team.

  • Take ownership of a defined functional role
  • Collaborate closely with the core leadership team
  • Help build, ship, and grow The Code Society
  • Direct mentorship and cross-team initiative leadership
