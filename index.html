The Code Society

01 / About us

We’re here to break the experience paradox.

Companies want freshers with 1–2 years of experience. But how are you supposed to gain experience when every opportunity already expects you to have it? Make it make sense.

That’s why The Code Society is here. We’re building a space where students can get hands-on experience by contributing to open-source projects, collaborating with other builders, and learning by doing.

You’ll navigate unfamiliar codebases, figure out tools you’ve never used, collaborate with a team, solve real problems, and ship actual work. You don’t need to know everything before you start. You just need to be willing to explore, learn, and do the work.

Show up curious. Leave with something built.
