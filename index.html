01 / The reason we exist
The experience paradox

You shouldn't need experience to get your first experience.

Students are expected to have practical experience before they are given opportunities to gain it. We want to help break that cycle. The Code Society connects learners with real repositories, meaningful projects, and a community where people can learn by contributing — not just by watching from the sidelines.
