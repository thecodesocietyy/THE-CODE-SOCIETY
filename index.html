ABOUT THE CODE SOCIETY

A BETTER WAY TO GET REAL EXPERIENCE.

WE MAKE ROOM FOR PEOPLE TO BUILD, CONTRIBUTE AND GROW.

  • 01

    We connect students with real open-source projects and practical contribution opportunities.

  • 02

    We bring project administrators and contributors together to build and improve meaningful work.

  • 03

    We provide access to mentors, feedback and a community that supports continuous learning.

  • 04

    We help student work reach wider campus communities through advocates, visibility and recognition.
